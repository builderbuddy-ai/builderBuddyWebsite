Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 01, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes our policies on the collection, use and disclosure of your information when you use the Service and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Definitions

  • Company refers to Builder Buddy AI, part of Iguest Management Corp.
  • Service refers to the website at builderbuddy-ai.com and the WhatsApp messaging service.
  • Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

Data We Collect

We may collect: email address, first and last name, phone number, usage data, and information you provide through WhatsApp including construction project details, expense records, and uploaded files (receipts or spreadsheets).

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To send and receive messages via WhatsApp using third-party providers (such as Twilio)
  • To process project-related data and store it in user-specific spreadsheets (Google Sheets)
  • To contact you about updates and service information
  • To manage your requests

SMS and WhatsApp Messaging

By using our Service, you consent to receive WhatsApp messages from Builder Buddy. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Standard message and data rates may apply.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, share, or rent consumer data or opt-in information.

Third-Party Services

We may share your information with: Twilio (for WhatsApp/SMS delivery) and Google (for spreadsheet data storage). We do not share data with any other third parties without your consent.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 13.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at johnny@builderbuddy-ai.com.
